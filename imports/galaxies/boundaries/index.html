How to play

Divide the board. One dot in each shape.

●

Each shape must fit itself after a half-turn around its dot.

Tap a line — add or remove a boundary.

Drag — draw several.

Tap inside a shape — check its half-turn.

Keyboard

Tab to a boundary. Enter or Space changes it. Arrow keys move between boundaries. R checks its shape. Z undoes. Escape cancels a drag.
