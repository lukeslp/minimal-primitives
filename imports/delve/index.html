Delve, an endless dungeon

Depth1 +1 +1

Arrow keys step. Q, E, Z and C step diagonally. Period or Enter waits a turn. F drinks a flask. N starts a new run.

How to play

Find the stairs and go deeper. An enemy marks the square it will strike next: step off it, then hit back.

Enemies keep in packs, and a pack wakes together when you see one of them. The pips under an enemy are the hits it can take. A heavy cross strikes for two.

A blade adds one to every hit. Armor adds a square of health. Walk over either to take it. You can carry two flasks.

On the board you are a small figure. A blot is a low mound with eyes and strikes your square; a lancer is a hooded figure with a spear and strikes two squares in a line; a hulk is broad and horned and strikes every square around it. A cross marks a square about to be struck, a heavy cross a strike for two, and a line joins the squares of a lancer's thrust. Small squares under an enemy count the hits it can still take. Your square turns solid for a turn when a strike finds you. Steps mark the stairs. A bottle is a flask, a sword is a blade and a shield is armor.
