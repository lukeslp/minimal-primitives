How to play

Draw a diagonal line in every square. Each number sits on a corner and says how many lines touch it. The lines can't form a closed loop.

The pencil button opens Free draw: a blank board with no numbers and no rules. Every closed section fills with color, loops included. Pick the size at the top, up to 16 × 16.

A closed section fills with color once its lines are right. Those lines then can't change.

Tap a square to draw a line. Tap again to turn it, and once more to clear it.

Drag across squares to paint the same line in each.

Hold a drawn line, or right-click it, to lock it so it can't change. Do it again to unlock.

A number turns blue when exactly that many lines touch it. Until then it is red.

More controls

Set Input to Slash or Backslash in Settings to draw only that line. Hint marks a square and the numbers or lines that decide it. Press Hint again to make the move.

Keys: arrows move. Enter or Space changes the square. / and \ draw that line, and Delete or Backspace clears it. Hold Shift while you press arrows to paint. L locks or unlocks a drawn line. Z undoes. Escape cancels a drag.

For a bigger board, set Board size to Enlarge in Settings. Then set Input to Pan to drag the board around without drawing.
