How to play

Draw one diagonal per square. Each number counts touching lines. No loops.

Free draw, the pencil beside Settings, is a blank board with no numbers or rules: every closed section fills with colour, loops included. Its size is chosen at the top, up to 16 × 16.

Color fills closed sections when their touching numbers match.

Tap — turn or clear a diagonal.

Drag — paint a line of diagonals.

Right-click or hold a drawn diagonal to lock or unlock it.

Blue numbers match; red numbers need attention.

More controls

Choose Slash or Backslash in Settings to paint one direction. Hint outlines a diagonal and the clues or path that force it.

Hint previews a move. Tap it again to apply.

Arrows move; Enter or Space cycles. / and \ draw; Delete clears. Shift + arrows paints. L locks or unlocks a drawn diagonal. Z undoes. Escape cancels a gesture.

For a larger board, choose Enlarge, then Pan in Settings.
