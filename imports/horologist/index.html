    Help

    Place wheels so every clock runs forwards.

    Tap a hole to place a wheel, and tap a wheel you placed to take it out or drag it to another hole.

    Keys: arrows pick a hole, Enter places or lifts a wheel, 1, 2 and 3 pick the size (smallest first), and Z undoes.
