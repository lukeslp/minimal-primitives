    Help

    Put wheels in the holes so every clock runs forwards: only errors use color, with broken red rings for backwards clocks and heavy red edges for jams.

    Tap a hole to place a wheel, or a loose wheel to lift it.

    Drag to move; arrows and Enter place, 1–3 choose a size, and Z undoes.
