      How to play

      Turn tiles until every line closes.

      Tap to turn clockwise, Shift-click to turn back, drag or scroll to turn either way, and hold or right-click a tile to lock it.

      Arrows choose a tile; Enter or Space turns it, Shift with Enter turns back, L locks it, Z, U or Backspace undoes, and H marks a hint, then makes its turn.
