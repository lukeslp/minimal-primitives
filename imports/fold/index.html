How to play

Fold the paper until a dot shows through every hole. A fold flips one side over onto the other, so each hole has to land on a dot. A dot that ends up face down shows faintly through the paper and doesn’t count. Fold it again to turn it face up. You can fold the smaller side of a line, or either side when both are the same size.

Drag a square across a fold line. The paper follows your finger. Let go once it passes the line to fold.

Or tap a square, then a highlighted fold line.

Each arrow on the edge folds along its line. If both sides could fold, it asks which one.

To unfold, swipe the folded side back the way it came, or press the undo arrow on its edge. Undo does the same.

Hint shows a fold. Press Hint again to make it. If no fold can finish the board, Hint points at Undo instead.

More controls

Tab to an arrow on the edge. Enter folds the side that fits. If both sides fit, Enter asks which one, and Shift+Enter folds the right or bottom side.

Arrow keys move between squares. Enter or Space picks a square. Z undoes a fold. Escape drops the picked square or closes this panel.

Restart unfolds the whole sheet, and Undo brings your folds back. After a solve you see how many folds you used, plus the fewest possible if you used more.

Finish all 25 boards to open New Game+. It gives you the 25 boards left out of the campaign, in random order, then makes new ones at the Difficulty you pick in Settings.
