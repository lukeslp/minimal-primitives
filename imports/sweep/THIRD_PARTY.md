# Local dependencies

Everything Sweep runs is in this folder. Nothing loads from another origin.

- **IBM Plex Mono and Plex Sans**, by IBM, SIL Open Font License 1.1; see `fonts/OFL.txt`. Unmodified copies of the files Glance ships.

`engine.js` and `boards.js` are byte-for-byte copies of Glance's, by the same author, so both games play the same fifty boards.
