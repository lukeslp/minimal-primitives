    How to play

    Press any key, or tap anywhere, as the frame passes your row, then again as it passes your tile.

    A tile turns over with its neighbours; make every tile dark in as few presses as you can.

    Undo and Hint are the last row; in step-scan, Space or the left half moves the frame and Enter or the right half chooses.

    Luke Steuber
