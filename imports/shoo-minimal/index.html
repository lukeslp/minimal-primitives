How to play

Tap a cell. Every bee beside it hops one cell straight away from the tap.

Tap a bee and it dodges and stays put. The bees beside it still hop away.

Get every bee into the hive in as few taps as you can. The top line shows the board, then your taps and par.

A bee can’t hop onto a flower, another bee or a hatched gap, or across a wall or off the edge. If it can’t hop, it stays where it is. So a bee on the edge can only move along it.

Undo takes back one tap. Restart puts the bees back at the start, and Undo after a restart brings your taps back.

More controls

Hint puts a dashed ring on the next tap of a shortest way home. Press Hint again to play it. If the bees can no longer all get home, Hint says so, and pressing it again undoes your last tap.

Keys: arrows move, Home and End jump to the ends of a row, Enter or Space taps, Z, U or Backspace undoes, and H hints.
