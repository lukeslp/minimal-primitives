How to play

Tap a cell. Every bee beside it hops one cell straight away from the tap.

Tap a bee and it dodges and stays put, while the bees beside it flee.

Bring every bee into the hive, in as few taps as you can. The count at the top is taps and par.

Flowers, walls, other bees, hatched gaps and the edge stop a bee; a bee that cannot go stays. A bee on the edge can only slide along it.

Undo takes back one tap. Restart sets the board up again, and Undo brings it back.

More controls

Hint marks one tap on a shortest way home with a dashed ring. Tap Hint again to play it. When no way home is left, Hint says so and its second tap undoes.

Arrows move. Enter or Space taps. Z or Backspace undoes. H hints.
