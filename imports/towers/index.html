How to play

Raise a tower on every square. Each row and each column holds every height once.

A number at the edge counts the towers seen from there. A taller tower hides the shorter ones behind it.

Tap — raises a tower by one. After the tallest, the square is empty again.

Hint — shows the next step and why. Press it again to fill it.

Drag sideways — turns the board, so any side can face you. The turn button does a quarter.

Black-topped towers are fixed. A dashed mark means a repeat or a miscount.

More controls

On a solved board, tap a number to see from there: the towers it counts stay lit and the ones behind a taller tower fall into shadow. V walks round the numbers; Escape rests.

[ and ] turn the board. Arrows move as it now faces. Enter or Space raises a tower. A digit sets a height; 0 or Delete clears. Z or Backspace undoes. H hints.

Restart clears this puzzle; Undo restores it.

Settings has a flat view: the same board as a plain grid of numbers.
