How to play

Raise a tower on every square. Each row and each column holds every height once.

A number at the edge counts the towers seen from there. A taller tower hides the shorter ones behind it.

Tap a square to raise its tower by one. After the tallest height, the square goes back to empty.

Hold a square to bring up a row of heights under the board without raising it. On a touch screen, a tap brings up the row too. Tap a height to set it, or the empty square to clear.

Hint shows the next step and why. Press Hint again to fill it in.

Drag sideways to turn the board, so you can look along any side. The turn button turns it a quarter.

Fixed towers have a solid top with the number in reverse. A dashed outline on a tower means its height repeats in its row or column. A dashed circle around a number means the count from there is wrong.

More controls

On a solved board, tap a number to look from there. The towers it counts stay lit, and the ones hidden behind taller towers go dark. V moves to the next number, and Escape turns the view off.

[ and ] turn the board. The arrow keys move the way the board now faces. Enter or Space raises a tower. A digit sets a height, and 0 or Delete clears it. Z or Backspace undoes. H hints.

Restart clears this board. Undo puts your towers back.

Set View to Flat in Settings to see the same board as a plain grid of numbers.
