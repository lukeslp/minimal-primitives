    How to play

    Follow a ring with your eyes, your head or a pointer until its trail fills, and it comes to rest in the picture.

    Fill every ring in as few choices as you can; a wrong one is crossed out, and the drifting undo takes it back.

    Camera frames never leave this device and nothing is stored; the camera stops when you leave or choose Pointer.
