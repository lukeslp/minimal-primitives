      How to play

      Tap a crossing to swap which thread lies on top; a knot pins its crossing, and the thread wearing the knot stays on top.

      Along each straight row and column, no thread may stay over or under for three crossings. Two alike is fine; three is not. A dashed line marks a loose stretch.

      Two equal crossings force the third of three to be opposite. Hint outlines that pair and the crossing it forces; press again to flip it.

      Use the check button to mark crossings you have settled, including ones that need no flip. A small square is your note; a round knot is fixed. Turn the check button off to resume normal play. A marked crossing must be unmarked before it can flip. Marks are optional and cost no hits.

      Arrows select; Enter or Space acts; L marks or unmarks; Z, U or Backspace undoes; H hints. Right-click also marks. The sixteen main boards finish the course; More cloths in Settings is optional.
