    How to play

    Each crossing has one thread on top. Tap a crossing to put the other thread on top. A dot is a knot. You can’t flip a knotted crossing, and the thread with the dot stays on top.

    No thread may go over three crossings in a row, or under three in a row. Two is fine. A dashed line marks a thread that breaks this rule. Clear every dashed line to finish the cloth.

    Hint circles a crossing to flip, and usually puts squares on the two matching crossings that force it. Press Hint again to flip it.

    The check button turns on marking. Tap a crossing to put a small square on it, and tap again to take it off. You can’t flip a marked crossing until you unmark it, though Hint will flip it if your mark was wrong. Marks are optional and don’t count as flips. Press the check button again to go back to flipping.

    The top line shows the board, then your flips and par, the fewest flips that solve it. Keys: arrows move, Enter or Space flips (or marks while marking is on), L marks, H hints, and Z, U or Backspace undoes. Right-click also marks. Finish all 16 boards to open New Game+ in Settings: 44 more cloths, then endless new ones.
