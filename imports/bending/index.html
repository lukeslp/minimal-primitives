Bending

Tab or a number goes to a planet. Enter places it, arrows move it, Delete sends it back. Z undoes, R restarts, H hints, N goes on.

How to play

Bend every beam into the light with its number.

A planet pulls a beam toward it, as far as the dots draw in around it.

Tap a planet
picks it
Tap a dot
moves it there
Drag
moves it; off the well sends it back
