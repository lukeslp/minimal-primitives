Bending

Tab or a number key selects a planet. Enter or Space places a waiting planet, arrows move it from dot to dot, and Delete or Backspace sends it back. Z or U undoes, R restarts, H gives a hint and N goes to the next stage.

How to play

Bend every beam into the light with its number.

A planet pulls on any beam that passes near it. The dots around a planet are drawn in toward it as far as its pull reaches. Past that, beams fly straight.
