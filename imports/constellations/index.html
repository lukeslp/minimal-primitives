Constellations

Each tick on a star is a thread it still needs, and you’re done when every star is a plain dot. A ring means a star has too many. Threads can’t cross, and the stars don’t all have to join up.

Tap a dotted line to put a thread on it. Tap again for a second thread, and a third time to clear it. If either star has no ticks left, the second tap clears it instead.

You can also drag from star to star, or tap one star and then another. Hold a thread to lock it so taps can’t change it, and hold it again to unlock it. Keys: arrows move between stars, Enter or Space picks one, Shift + arrow changes the line in that direction, L locks or unlocks a star’s threads, Z undoes and Escape lets go.

Hint marks a line that needs a thread, or one with a thread too many, and says why. Press Hint again to play it.
