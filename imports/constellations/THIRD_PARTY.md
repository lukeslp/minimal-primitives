# Notice

The twelve boards (`boards.js`) and the reveal's star list (`sky.json`) are derived from d3-celestial by Olaf Frohn: the constellation line figures (`constellations.lines.json`), their names (`constellations.json`) and the stars to magnitude 6 (`stars.6.json`). `development/real-sky.cjs` rebuilds both files from that data.

The star data in d3-celestial comes from XHIP: An Extended Hipparcos Compilation. Anderson, E. and Francis, C. (2012), Astronomy Letters 38, 331. VizieR catalog V/137D.
