    How to play

    Hold your look on a point until its ring fills, or tap it, to set the pen down; then look or drag from point to point.

    Ink every grey line once, visiting as few points as you can; rest your look off the points, or tap between them, to lift the pen.

    Camera frames never leave this device and nothing is stored; the camera stops when you leave or choose Pointer.
