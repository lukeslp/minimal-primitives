How to play

Drag cubes from the tray onto the floor until every shadow fills its outline.

Drag a stacked cube to move it, or off the floor to put it back.

Tap or Enter adds; wheel up adds and down returns; arrows choose or carry, Space lifts or drops, Escape cancels, Shift–Enter returns, Z/U/Backspace undoes, and H marks then places a hint.
