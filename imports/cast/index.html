    How to play

    Stack cubes from the tray until every shadow fills its outline; each solve opens the next of thirty boards, and after the last comes New Game+.

    Drag a top cube to move it; drag it off, hold it or right-click to put it back; drag bare floor sideways to look behind tall stacks.

    Tap or Enter adds, Delete removes, Space lifts and drops, [ and ] swing the view, Z undoes, H hints.
