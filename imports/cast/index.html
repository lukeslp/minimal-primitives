    How to play

    Drag cubes from the tray onto the floor until every shadow on the walls and the floor fills its outline; solve all 25 boards to open New Game+.

    Tap to add a cube, drag a top cube to move it, and drag it off the room, hold it or right-click it to put it back in the tray; drag sideways on empty floor to look behind tall stacks.

    Keys: arrows choose a square, Enter adds, Delete removes, Space picks up and drops, [ and ] turn the view, H shows a hint and sets it on a second press, and Z undoes.
