How to play

Light up every open square. A bulb lights its row and column until a wall stops it. Two bulbs can’t shine on each other.

Tap a square for a bulb. Tap again for ×, which marks it empty, and once more to clear it. Drag to paint every square you cross the same way as the first.

Hold a square, or right-click it, to mark it × straight away. The Mark empty button (a box with an ×) swaps the two: taps mark ×, and hold or right-click places a bulb.

Lighter panels are lit; darker panels still need light.

More controls

A number on a wall needs exactly that many bulbs in the squares touching its sides. A white number still needs more, a blue one has the right count, and a yellow one has too many. The soft glow on diagonal squares doesn’t count as light.

Hint circles a square and outlines the squares that explain the move. Press Hint again to make it. If one of your marks is wrong, Hint circles that mark and says why.

Keys: arrows move, and Home and End jump to the ends of a row. Enter or Space cycles a square, or marks it × while Mark empty is on. X marks ×, Delete clears, and Z undoes. Escape cancels a drag. Restart clears the whole board, and Undo brings it back.

On a big board, set Board size to Enlarge in Settings, then set Input to Pan and drag the board around.
