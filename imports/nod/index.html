    How to play

    To tip the trays, tilt your head to one side and hold, then bring it back to level before the next tip; you can also drag, swipe, tap beside the marble or press an arrow key.

    The marble slides until a block or the edge stops it, or until it falls through a hole to the tray below, and you finish by getting it into the one hole on the bottom tray.

    Camera frames never leave this device and are never saved, and the camera stops when you leave the page or choose Pointer.
