    Help

    Swipe the way you want the marble to roll, or tap on that side of it. It rolls until a block or the edge stops it.

    When it rolls onto a hole, it falls to the same spot on the tray below and stops. If it lands on another hole, it falls again.

    Get it into the hole on the bottom tray to finish the board.

    Keys: arrows or W, A, S and D tilt, and Z or Backspace undoes. Hint shows the next tilt. Press Hint again to make it.
