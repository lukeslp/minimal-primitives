How to play

Get the marble out of the cube. After every move it falls straight down as far as the tubes let it. It can only leave through the end with the double ring, and only when that end faces down.

Drag across the cube to turn one layer. Swipe or tap beside the cube to roll the whole cube that way.

To turn the view, drag with two fingers, or right-drag with a mouse. Pinch or scroll to zoom.

More controls

The top line shows the board number, your moves and par.

Solve all 25 boards to open New Game+, with 25 more boards in random order. Start it from the arrow after a solve, or from Settings.

Hint shows a good next move. Press Hint again to play it. If you need to back up first, it lights Undo instead.

The arrow keys roll the cube toward that side of the screen. F, B, L, R, U and D turn the front, back, left, right, top and bottom layers clockwise, as seen from that side. Hold Shift to turn the other way. On a 3 by 3 by 3 cube, M, C and S turn the middle layers, the same way as L, D and F. Q and E turn the view, + and − zoom, and 0 resets the view. Z or Backspace undoes. H shows a hint.

One switch (Settings, Access): the highlight lights the four arrows together first. Press to pick them, then press again when the arrow you want is lit, and the cube rolls that way. The drag key comes next. Two moving lines set where the drag starts, two more set where it ends, and a drag across the cube turns a layer. Undo, Hint and Next come last.
