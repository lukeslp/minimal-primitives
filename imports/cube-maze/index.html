How to play

The marble falls straight down after every move and leaves only when the double ring faces down. Bring it out.

Drag the cube to turn a layer, or swipe beside it to roll the whole cube.

Drag with two fingers (or right-drag) to look around; pinch or scroll to zoom.

More controls

The top line shows the board, then moves made and par.

Hint shows a next move; press it again to play it.

The arrow keys roll the cube toward that side of the screen. F, B, L, R, U and D turn the front, back, left, right, top and bottom layers; on a three-cubie cube M, C and S turn the middle ones. Shift turns the other way. Q and E turn the view, + and − zoom, and 0 resets it. Z or Backspace undoes; H asks for a hint.

One switch (Settings, Access): the highlight first lights the four arrows together. Press to choose them, then press again when the arrow you want is lit; it rolls the cube that way. The drag key comes next: two gliding lines set where the drag starts, two more set where it ends, and a drag across the cube turns a layer. Undo, Hint and Next come last.
