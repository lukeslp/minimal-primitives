Help

Light up every open square. A bulb lights its row and column until a wall stops it. Two bulbs can’t shine on each other. A numbered wall needs exactly that many bulbs in the squares touching its sides.

Tap a square, or press Enter or Space, to place or remove a bulb. To mark a square empty with ×, right-click it, press X, or turn on the × button and tap. Drag to paint every square you cross the same way as the first. Arrows move, Delete clears a square, and Z undoes.

Hint circles a square and outlines the squares that explain the move. Press Hint again to make it. If one of your marks breaks a rule, Hint names the rule and circles a mark to fix.

On a big board, set Board size to Enlarge in Settings, then set Input to Pan and drag the board around. Escape cancels a drag before you let go, and Undo takes back a whole drag.

Akari, also called Light Up, follows the pencil-puzzle rules published by Nikoli. These boards are original.
