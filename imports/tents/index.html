How to play

Pitch one tent beside every tree. Each tent belongs to one tree. Tents never touch, not even at a corner.

Each number counts the tents in its row or column.

Tap: tent, then a dot, then blank. A dot is your note that a cell stays empty. Drag across cells to dot them; start the drag on a dot to clear dots.

A short line ties each tent to a tree beside it. A number turns gray when its line is exact and is boxed when it has too many. Tents that break a rule are hatched.

More controls

Hint shows the next cell that can be worked out and says why. Tap it again to apply.

Arrows move. Enter or Space cycles a cell. Z or Backspace undoes. H hints. Restart clears the board; Undo brings it back.
