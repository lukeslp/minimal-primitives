    How to play

    One switch: any key, or a tap or click anywhere. [ and ] change the speed.

    Every cell is a clock. Press as a clock’s hand reaches the top to fill that cell, or to empty it. Undo has a clock too. When you choose by line, you open a row first, then pick a cell in it.

    The numbers beside each row and above each column are the runs of filled cells in that line, in order. A line’s numbers dim once its cells match them. Match every line to finish the picture.
