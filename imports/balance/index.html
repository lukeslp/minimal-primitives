How to play

Make every square black or white. Never three alike in a row, across or down. Every row and column holds as many black as white.

Tap — black → white → empty. A square with sharp corners is fixed. The ones you place have rounded corners.

A small x past the end of a row or column marks a broken rule there: three alike, or too many of one shade.

Hint shows a square the rules force. Press it again to fill it.

Keys — arrows move, Enter or Space changes a square, Z undoes, H hints.
