The library marks four more finished games: Tangles and Horologist show their solved board, Bending turns its tile color, and Hexsweeper turns color once every sphere size is cleared. Tilt and Shoo have more room under the board for the Next button. Tapping the board number at the top of a game opens its board list, also on phones with a notch. Saved boards and progress are kept. Still no camera input and no camera permission in this build.
